The research

Dean Ammons’s four-paper Information Flow Series examines behavioral influence, confidential information recovery, provenance, and the limits of intervention. It connects formal questions about information with fictional thought experiments about credit, power, dependency, and responsibility.

The papers ask what a system can retain or disclose, what evidence can identify an origin, and what an intervention must reach when copies and delegated actions survive.

Reading the work

The research, publication records, and public discussions are collected here. The papers can be assessed through their definitions, assumptions, arguments, methods, and available materials.

How to engage

Precise criticism is welcome. Identify the claim, the relevant source passage, and the observation or counterexample that would change the conclusion. For a proposed experiment, specify the comparison, the control, and what the result would establish.
