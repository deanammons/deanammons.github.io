Reading the evidence

Different questions need different tests.

The series includes proposed mathematical frameworks, constructed examples, an in-context pilot, and a restricted small-model experiment. Those forms of evidence support different conclusions.

Individual paper pages explain what was measured, what was assumed, and which further tests would matter. A DOI records a publication; it is not evidence of independent review or replication.

Paper I’s public repository includes source, a protocol, retained responses, and checking programs. The other releases provide manuscripts and explanatory material; complete experimental reproduction packages are not supplied for every paper.
