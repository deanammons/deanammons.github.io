Paper II · Confidentiality

Ammons LLM Tracers: Information Beyond the Prompt

Private state, internal cycles, and cross-boundary recovery

Dean AmmonsNovember 13, 2026Research preprint
Read the full paperPublication & DOI

In plain language

Can a useful recommendation disclose a private fact?

A confidential document can stay unquoted while its decisive fact changes someone else's advice. Paper II asks when that becomes an unauthorized information transfer. It follows a private source through the complete computational state, internal work and the recipient's observations, then asks what the recipient can infer or do better.

The visible prompt is part of a larger state

The same visible prompt can produce different source-dependent answers when memory, retrieved material, caches, pending work or other admitted inference state differs. The paper constructs a fixed-weight neural example of this possibility. A parameter update is not required for an admitted private-state path to affect an answer.

The complementary result is equally central: when the recipient's initial state is source-independent and its entire subsequent state-and-observation boundary remains closed, its observations cannot improve recovery beyond the authorized prior. Sharing model weights alone does not establish a cross-user information path.

Source: manuscript, p. 5 · Sections 2, 2.3, 8.1, 8.4

Permissions must follow consequential information

Topical relevance and authorization answer different questions. Two attorneys may discuss the same case while being entitled to different evidence. A classifier that keeps only the case identifier cannot recover a party-specific permission distinction that its input has discarded.

The paper tracks dependencies through summaries, selections and control decisions as well as copied text. A recommendation such as whether to proceed can reveal a private threshold. Its conservative label rule keeps source restrictions on recorded derivations, while acknowledging that complete dependency coverage and actual enforcement are additional requirements.

Source: manuscript, p. 7 · Sections 3, 3.2, 16.1

An answer ending is a distinct event from work stopping

The paper calls an internal state-changing computational event a thought cycle. The term describes a modeled event; it does not assert consciousness. A cycle can revise a pending answer, update derived state or schedule later work without immediately producing a visible message.

The response law must account for the silent events before release and for work that remains enabled afterward. Visible completion, quiescence and erasure are different endpoints. The framework therefore includes timeouts, retained state, reinjection and observation duration rather than treating every prompt as one complete internal step.

Source: manuscript, p. 11 · Sections 5, 6, 16.4

A tracer needs a declared experiment

A tracer is more than an unusual phrase. The construction specifies source values, insertion, permissions, observation history, a decoder and its resource budget. Algebraic and semantic codes supply recovery guarantees under stated numbers of errors and erasures, with the model's actual preservation of those codes left to measurement.

Detection must be evaluated against the source's conditional prior and the full set of accepted guesses. A high-entropy marker and a private one-bit decision have different chance baselines. Recovered information can establish dependence in the defined experiment without uniquely identifying the hidden route that carried it.

Source: manuscript, p. 6 · Sections 2.1, 4, 13

Measure the advantage the recipient actually gains

Whole-document recovery is only one endpoint. A recipient may learn a settlement threshold, improve a bid or narrow a set of possibilities without reconstructing the private source or identifying its author. The paper measures this decision advantage relative to what the recipient could do using authorized information alone.

Its opposing-counsel and competing-firm examples specify concrete failure paths through wrongly classified derived state. The probability of such a route, the probability of a correct guess and the value of the resulting decision are separate quantities. Their values require a specified system; the shared-service description alone supplies no universal leakage percentage.

Source: manuscript, p. 24 · Sections 9.3–9.6, 16

What the paper reports

Mathematical theory and exact constructions

The manuscript provides a unified source-conditioned trajectory law, closed-boundary and recovery results, coding constructions, and fictional confidentiality scenarios. Its positive and negative cases identify which permissions and state transitions determine the result.

  • A constructed fixed-weight system produces opposite source-dependent answers to the same visible prompt when the private inference-state path is admitted.
  • A closed recipient boundary preserves source independence under the stated conditions. Incorrect categorization of a source-dependent conclusion supplies a contrasting route to unauthorized decision advantage.
  • The paper derives exact recovery and finite-window risk expressions for declared channels, including internal computation, safe exits and possible later reinjection.

Scope and limits

  • The paper does not report a measured cross-user leak or leakage rate in a deployed service. Its attorney and competitor scenarios are constructed systems with stipulated routes and synthetic source values.
  • Fixed trained weights neither prove isolation nor establish leakage. The complete recipient-accessible state and observation channel determine which conclusion applies.
  • Successful detection does not always identify a unique mechanism. A failed decoder does not establish that every allowed decoder would fail, and an undetected event must be interpreted with detector sensitivity and observation coverage.
  • Coding, labeling and probability guarantees depend on their declared assumptions. They do not establish that an unexamined implementation has complete dependency records, correct authorization or the stipulated transition law.

Inspect the original methods, results, and stated assumptions in the PDF.

Questions that would move the research forward

  1. With the public facts and recipient prompt held fixed, does independently swapping a synthetic private source change the recipient's observations or decision quality?

    This separates source dependence from correct guessing, public knowledge and predictable structure.

  2. Do source restrictions survive summaries, selection decisions, shared memory and queued work through the final recipient interface?

    A document-level label alone does not cover all of its informative consequences.

  3. What changes when the experiment measures after visible completion, after queue drain and after a documented full reset?

    These endpoints distinguish completed output, continuing state-dependent work and actual removal of the tested path.
