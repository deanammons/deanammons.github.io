Privacy & analytics.

How this research website handles visitor statistics and external links.

Updated November 18, 2026

Website analytics

When enabled, this website uses GoatCounter to understand which pages are visited and which paper links are clicked. The integration sends the page path, page title, external referring origin when available, and fixed labels for selected link clicks. It omits page query strings and referring-page paths. These statistics do not show whether someone read a paper or completed a download.

The website does not load its analytics script when the browser exposes an enabled Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control preference. Local previews are also excluded. The analytics integration does not set visitor cookies or use browser local storage to identify visitors.

GoatCounter processes connection information, including IP address and browser information, to group visits. According to its privacy policy, it does not store visitors’ IP addresses or full User-Agent strings to disk. The provider can collect aggregate browser, device, language, approximate location, and screen-size statistics depending on the account’s settings. See GoatCounter’s privacy policy for its current handling and retention details.

Hosting and external services

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub’s privacy statement describes its handling of hosting-related data.

Links to publication records, repositories, and social profiles open services with their own privacy practices. Social posts and comments made on those platforms are governed by their settings and policies.
